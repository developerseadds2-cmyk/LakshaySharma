### Important corrections from cross-checking

The official Badminton Association of India results confirm that his 2017 Coimbatore U-13 gold was in boys' doubles, partnered with Ankit Mondal, rather than singles. A separate 2017 report confirms that he and Ankit won silver at the Jaipur All-India Sub-Junior Ranking Tournament. These clarify two entries above, and bring the documented silver finishes to at least six.&#x20;

These records establish a substantial medal history, but they are not yet a complete, officially verified lifetime tally, particularly for his early state and junior events.

For a professional player profile or achievement poster, I would feature his 2026 double championship gold, 2025 national senior silver, and international medals from Kazakhstan, Poland, Czech Republic, Cameroon and Uganda as the headline achievements.
